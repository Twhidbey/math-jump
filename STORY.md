# Math Ninja: the story

This file is the one source for the story. Every bit of story text in `index.html` should match it.

**Keep it very simple.** The players are young kids (kindergarten to about 3rd grade). Each page is 1–3 short sentences; each picture caption is a few words. No mystery and no clues: Kuro is Kai's brother from the first page. (Nov 2026: the owner asked for this; the older version had a mystery picture, four boss clues, a mask reveal, a wooden ninja and a headband. All cut.) Every story page has a 🔊 Read to me button.

## The heart of it

- **Theme:** you don't have to earn love. A father who never stops loving the son who ran away (the prodigal son). A little brother who saves the brother who fought him. A welcome home.
- **Kai** is Sensei's youngest son. **Kuro** is his big brother, who took the five Math Scrolls, ran away and became the **Shadow Shogun**. **Sensei** wants both sons home.
- The Golem, Mega-Bot and the Snowman turn good when Kai is kind to them. Kage runs away.
- The last right answer in every boss fight is a hand held out: "Take my hand?"

## In the boss fights

- **Before the fight (taunts):** Kage "No one gets past my blade... unless they can add!" · Golem "ROCK SMASH! You will never take away my scroll!" · Mega-Bot "BEEP BOOP. CALCULATING... YOUR DEFEAT." · Snowman "ROOOAR! Everyone runs from me. You will too!" · Shogun "Go home, little brother!"
- **After the last answer** (Kai first says "Take my hand?"):
  - Kage: "Never!" and he fades away.
  - Golem: Kai "You don't have to be angry." Golem "Thank you, little ninja." ♥
  - Mega-Bot: Kai "You can rest now." Bot "...REST?" then "EVERYONE REST!" ♥
  - Snowman: Kai "Nobody should be alone." Snowman "You're not running away?" ♥ "A friend!"
  - Shogun: "Why... why didn't you finish me?" Kai "Look out!" (the beam falls, then the ending pages).

## Lost friends (optional, one per world)

Five little students from Sensei's dojo: **Hana** (Japan), **Tomo** (Lava), **Niko** (Robot), **Yuki** (Snow) and **Mei** with her lamb (Castle). Lines: "Help!" (near the secret), "Help me, Kai!", "Thank you, Kai!", "See you at the dojo!". The Welcome Home page shows the ones found: "Hana, Tomo came home too!" or, with all five, "Every one came home!" (Mei going after her one lost lamb is a quiet nod to the lost sheep.)

## Home screen

The scroll row under the buttons has one slot per world; beating a boss fills it with that world's scroll. Tapping a scroll opens that part of the story.

## Races (one line each, at the start of a travel race)
- **Red Bandit:** one of Kage's bandits.
- **Shadow Rider:** the Shogun's scout ("He tried to stop you the whole way").
- **Gray Wolf:** a wild wolf who just loves to race.
