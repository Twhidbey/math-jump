# Math Ninja: the story

This file is the one source for the story. Every bit of story text in `index.html` should match it.

**Keep it very simple.** The players are young kids (kindergarten to about 3rd grade). Each page is 1–3 short sentences; each picture caption is a few words. No mystery and no clues: Kuro is Kai's brother from the first page. (Nov 2026: the owner asked for this; the older version had a mystery picture, four boss clues, a mask reveal, a wooden ninja and a headband. All cut.) Every story page has a 🔊 Read to me button.

## The heart of it

- **Theme:** you don't have to earn love. A father who never stops loving the son who ran away (the prodigal son). A little brother who saves the brother who fought him. A welcome home.
- **Kai** is Sensei's youngest son. **Kuro** is his big brother, who took the seven Math Scrolls, ran away and became the **Shadow Shogun**. **Sensei** wants both sons home.
- The Golem, Mega-Bot, the Snowman, the Octopus and the Scorpion turn good when Kai is kind to them. Kage runs away.
- The last right answer in every boss fight is a hand held out: "Take my hand?"

- **Before the fight (taunts):** Kage "No one gets past my blade... unless they can add!" · Golem "ROCK SMASH! You will never take away my scroll!" · Mega-Bot "BEEP BOOP. CALCULATING... - **After the last answer** (Kai first says "Take my hand?"):
  - Kage: "Never!" then "Kuro paid us a scroll to be his friends. Ha!" and he fades away.
  - Golem: Kai "You don't have to be angry." Golem "Thank you, little ninja." ♥ "Kuro said never let go of my anger. It only made me hurt more."
  - Mega-Bot: Kai "You can rest now." Bot "KURO SAID: NEVER STOP. ...WE CAN REST?" then "EVERYONE REST!" ♥
  - Snowman: Kai "Nobody should be alone." Snowman "You're not running away?" ♥ "A friend!" then "Kuro said being alone makes you strong. I was just cold." "A friend!"
  - Octopus: Kai gives him one of his own gems: "Here. This gem is for you." Octopus "For me? Nobody ever gave me anything!" ♥ then "Here! Everyone gets a piece!" (he lets go of his whole hoard; little fish take the pieces home), then "Kuro said keep every piece. It was never enough." During the fight each hit makes him drop one piece.
  - Scorpion: Kai "I forgive you." Scorpion "But... I stung you!" then "Then I will put my stinger down." ♥ "Kuro taught me to sting first." (forgiveness: he stung first, and Kai forgives him anyway).
  - Shogun: "Why... why didn't you finish me?" Kai "Look out!" (the beam falls, then the ending pages).

## Why Kuro went to each world (owner, Nov 22 2026)

Each world is a place Kuro went looking for something to take the place of his father's love, and he traded one Math Scroll there to get it: **Village** popularity and friends · **Volcano** anger about past hurts · **Factory** control · **Snow** needing no one (pride) · **Sea** riches · **Desert** revenge · **Castle** putting himself on the throne. The world story page says it in one line ("Kuro came here to be popular."), each boss says it after being won over, and Kuro's journal says it in his own words.

## The world map

The road leaves the dojo (Home), goes round the seven lands in story order and comes back home (gold once Kuro is home). Kuro's footprints mark the road out. Plain "Unknown land" areas are left for worlds still to come. Places on the map (tap for a line): Bandit Camp, Frozen Falls, Sunken Ship, Suna's Cliff House, Moon Tower; and, for the story later: **Sensei's Lookout** ("Every night Sensei stood here with his lamp, watching the road for Kuro."), **Kite Hill** ("Kai and Kuro flew their kites here when they were small."), **The Crossroads** ("Here Kuro turned off the road home."), **Old Bridge**, **Lamb Meadow** ("A shepherd once searched here for one lost lamb."), **Lighthouse** ("Its light shows ships the way home."), **Old Mine**.

## Story in the levels

Right answers put right what Kuro spoiled: Village bandits drop their Kuro signs ("Friends!"), Volcano vents cool to springs ("Cool water!"), Factory robots are unchained ("Free!"), lone penguins join Kai ("Come along!"), Sea chests open and fish carry the gems off ("Share!"), Desert scorpions calm down ("Peace!"), Castle banners fall ("Down it comes!"). Kuro's notes on signposts: Village "My new friends like ME best! –K", Volcano "Leave me alone! –K", Factory "Everything here obeys me. –K", Snow "I don't need anyone. Go home! –K", Desert "They'll be sorry! –K", Castle "Bow to the king! –K"; third levels "Turn back, little brother! –K". Near the end of a level Kai glimpses Kuro: "Go home, Kai!" and he runs off. Bosses Kai won over help in later worlds: Golem "Up you come, little ninja!", Mega-Bot "REST WELL, KAI!", Snowman "I got him, Kai!", Octopus "Here, Kai! I share now!", Scorpion "I sting for you now!". Sea levels end with a fish feast: "A fish feast! Everyone shares."

## Home screen

The scroll row under the buttons has one slot per world; beating a boss fills it with that world's scroll. Tapping a scroll opens that part of the story.
