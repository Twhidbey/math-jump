# Math Ninja: handoff (read this first in a new chat)

Everything is in `index.html`. `CLAUDE.md` maps the code and records the owner's decisions; `STORY.md` is the story text; `README.md` is for people; `MATH_GOALS.md` holds the math-learning ideas. Work on **`main`** of this repo (`Twhidbey/math-jump`) and push there: pushing IS publishing (GitHub Pages). After every change: update the build stamp (search `class="build"`), commit, push, and tell the owner it's live (bump `?v=` on the link if the phone shows the old one). This repo is public: keep personal details out of the notes.

## The owner and players
- Players: young kids (kindergarten to about 3rd grade math), mostly on an iPhone. The lost-friend rescues are a favourite.
- Owner likes: ask before coding (short options + a recommendation), short kid-friendly text, pixel art (no emoji in the game), cohesion (plain blocks from each world), every right answer visibly doing something, Kai keeps moving, subtle Christian themes (grace, the lost sheep, welcome home).
- Rules to keep: nothing hangs from the sky (hang it from posts/roofs); no puzzle gates; no stomping math critters; the story stays very simple (Kuro is the brother from page one).

## Built Nov 7, 2026
- **New trick! cards**: the first time a question needs a trick the kid hasn't met (make 10, doubles, ×9, …), Sensei's card shows it with a sample fact as the question comes up. Once per trick per player.
- **Work it out with Sensei**: missing the same fact twice in one day (even in different levels) pauses the game and opens a card that walks through the trick in small steps the kid answers (8 + 5: 8 + ? = 10 → 5 is 2 and ? → 10 + 3 = ?). ✕ closes it any time. The tip card also has a **Show me** button. A worked-out fact gets no silver that level, so it keeps coming back.

- **Pixel art instead of emoji** in the Badge Book (21 icons), level hints (15), gem counts, streak, race timer, kick button, Pick your math and the Fact Book. Left for later (owner to decide): player animal icons (new art), Settings buttons, How to play rows, small labels.
- Sensei's (and Kai's) eye no longer turns into two pixels when he bows.
- **Rescue powers last only to the end of that level** (owner). Replaying a rescue gives the power again for that level, plus 5 gems. Hana's high kick still works in all of Japan (it's the rescue treasure, not the power).

- **Never get hurt** switch in Settings (per player, owner asked for a frustrated K kid): hits only make Kai blink and hop back, falls are free, no hearts lost.
