## Fractions (the sea's kind, `cat: 'frac'`)

Skills `fname` (3 of 4 parts, tier 2), `fname2` (5ths/6ths/8ths), `fcmp` (1/3 ? 1/4 → < = >), `fof` (1/3 of 12, needs `d2`), `feq` (1/2 = ?/4), `fadd` (1/5 + 2/5). A fraction answer is a **string** `'3/4'` (`isFrac`, `fracVal`, `fr`); questions with non-number answers carry `q.opts` (wrong answers; `fracOpts` never offers one worth the same), and `choices`/`rowChoices` use `optChoices`. Canvas text that is exactly `a/b` is drawn stacked by a `CanvasRenderingContext2D` `fillText`/`strokeText` wrapper (top of the script); buttons use `valHTML` (`.fr`). Not typed in the Daily Dojo / keypad. Pictures: `pic: { k: 'parts', bars: [[k, n], ...] }` (`drawTipPic`); `kidPic` shows a one-bar picture with every "N of M parts" question (in the sea the panel sits on the sand). Grade tracks: 'Fractions' in grades 2–5 (`GRADE_TRACKS`), `WORLD_HOME.sea = 'frac'`, PATH has them too
