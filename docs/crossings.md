## Gates

**A gate's question only shows near it** (owner, Nov 2026: a question hanging out far ahead, past other puzzles, was confusing): `gateNear(st)` = from `st.s − 3` (where the answer buttons show) to the gate. Before that the HUD banner is hidden (just "n / N gates"), the sign says "Gate n", answer blocks / sea doors show "?", and no New trick card. Sea gates have a short run-up (wall at `s + 8`), sign at `s + 2`. One answer per gate (`need: 1`). Opening per world: `gateKey` (lava world has one per level: Ash Trail stone `ash`, Lava Falls `lava`, Obsidian `obsidian`), `openGate`, `drawGateTile`, `drawGateFx`. Answering: `syncAnsBar`, `barTarget`, `answerTarget`, `tapAnswer`, `gateAnswer` → `st.star` → `resolveAnswer`; colours `ANS_COLS`/`ansCol`; phone buttons in an arc by the jump button; computers have no buttons (`isTouch`), keys , . / (N M , . / for five, `ANS_KEYS`) or 1–5, `keyTag` labels; Lightning bonus if answered before reaching the gate

## Skip-count stepping stones (one crossing in a stones level, `stepsAt = [1]`; see Level builder. **After the crossing** the stones fall one by one (`st.fall`, in `updateSteps`), break (`stepsBreak`) and fill their 3-column sections with `S` tiles (`stepsFill`); `drawSteps` paints the numbers on the filled floor; no plank bridge. Owner liked this from the dreamy-hamilton line)

Answer timer `STEP_TIME` (8 s, 12 s Easy; none in Easy mode for a K player or a tier-1 skill, `stepTimed`). Sign "Count up / back by Ns" with a green up / orange down pixel arrow (`drawSteps`), first line "Count up! Each one is N more.", each right hop shows `+N` / `−N` (`stepsPick`) drawn as a bar over the top choice in `drawSteps`, checked in `crossTick`; time out → `stepsCrumble` (all blocks crumble, Kai drops, counts as a miss, no heart). Crossing is a math move, not platforming: walking to the edge auto-hops Kai onto the first stone (`updateSteps` → `crossStart`), arrows and jump do nothing (jump shows "Pick a number!" and is cleared so it doesn't fire later; left on the first stone hops back), each answer hops him onto the picked stone (`stepsPick`, `crossHop`, `crossTick`; `G.cross`); wrong block crumbles → fall into the pit → `stepsCatch` puts Kai back at the start, new pattern, no heart. Blocks are plain squares in the world's stair style (choices in answer colours); owner rejected fancy oval stones and pit clouds. `stepSeq` (step follows the skill: 1s/2s for K, 5s/10s, the times table, counts back for take-away), `stepsSet`, `stepsOpts`, `stepsQ`, `stepsTarget`, `drawSteps`, `STONE_TOPS`, `stepX`. Normal 5 numbers (4 answers), easy 4

## Pattern walls (older players only: 2-digit adding and up, times / divide)

One per level, replaces the middle gate (`wallGate = feedAt` in `buildLevel`, `wallChunk`). `wallKinds(skill)`: times/divide (and Think tier 4+) get prime / pascal / fib, 2-digit adding gets pascal / fib, K skills none; picked by world × 3 + level. `wallPuzzle` builds cells (`x`, `y` rows up, `type` num/hole/fill); tiles `X` (wall block) and `Z` (choice block), both solid, drawn only in `drawWalls` (after `drawSteps`); `L.walls`, `L.wallCells` ("c,r" → cell). **Pascal's Triangle** (5–7 rows by tier, 2–3 holes that never touch) and **Fibonacci Stairs** (8–9 steps, 2–3 holes): three choice blocks hang over the run-up; bonk (`wallBonk`), tap (`wallTap`), buttons/keys (`wallTarget` via `miniTarget`, `wallPick`); the block flies into the hole (`w.fly`), parents flash, "3 + 3 = 6". A miss shows the sum, the block flies in anyway, no gems. **Prime Wall** (5 × 3, 5 primes, mostly odd composites): touch the wall itself (tap/click any block); buttons/keys point at 3 highlighted blocks (`w.opts`, one prime). Wrong: "Not prime: 9 = 3 × 3". All done → `wallDone` (pattern lights up 80 frames) → crumbles, `openGate(w)`, checkpoint. Sensei explains each kind once (`prog.wallSeen`). Facts are not tracked (`op: 'room'`). `buildLevel` runs before `G`/`isTouch` exist, so the build path must not touch them

## Swing crossings (ropes swing like pendulums; the one Kai grabs next swings out to his ledge, owner asked; one per level, before gate `def.n − 2`, then a `run()`)

`swingChunk` (two `S` steps up, a 2-wide `S` ledge 3 high at `l0`, 6-tile gap, the world pillar `O` perch at `pm`, 6-tile gap, ledge `l1`, steps down; `L.swings`). `updateSwings` starts it when Kai lands anywhere on the start ledge or leaps out over the gap from up there (kids run and jump); `G.swing` (`ph` ask / go, `half` 0–1), `swingTick` from the top of `updatePlayer` (twists pause), `swingTarget` via `miniTarget` (kind `'swing'` → `swingPick`), answer tags over the next gap (`g.tiles`, clickable). Right = swing `SWING_FRAMES` 64 across one half (dip 1.8 T; the ski lift 0.6 T); wrong = wobble, "Oops! a = b", new question after 90 frames, no penalty; no timer; **going back** (owner): ← on the start ledge steps back (`sw.rearm`: the swing waits until he leaves the ledge or walks at the gap again), ← on the perch swings him back to the start ledge (free, the question comes again next time); **a finished crossing swings freely both ways** (`swingFree(sw, dir)`, `g.free`/`g.dir`/`g.from`: walk at the gap from either ledge or jump out over it, no question, HUD "Swinging back!"). `swingCatch`: a fall into a gap puts Kai back on the start ledge, no heart; `hurt` ignores hits while swinging. `SWING_LOOK` per world (owner rule: nothing hangs from the sky, so everything hangs from posts on the ledges/perch): red torii beam + ropes, rock arch + chains, striped crane rail + hooks, ski lift (sagging cable, red chairs, stops at the middle tower), lantern rope roof to roof. `drawSwings` after `drawSteps`. Things kept off it: flavor, ? boxes, lost friends

## Ski lift (snow world's crossing, owner asked: low to high, ride it up, always running)

`liftChunk` in `buildLevel` (instead of `swingChunk` when `W.key === 'snow'`; only small `flat()` runs so the level's rng rolls don't change): boarding station on the ground (`sw.b`), 8 open tiles with a middle tower, a 6-block cliff (`sw.cl`, 4 wide, 'S') with the top station, stairs down. `sw.lift`; `LIFT_CHAIRS` 6 loop on `sw.ph` (0..1 up, 1..2 back down empty, `liftPos`, `liftSeat`), `LIFT_SPD` 1/360 per frame, moved every frame by `liftsMove` (in `updateSwings` / `swingTick`). `G.swing.lift`: phases ask (at the bottom; ← steps back) → board (hops into the next chair) → ride (2nd question asked at once; if it isn't answered the lift slows near the tower and waits at p 0.495) → off (hops onto the top station). `liftTick`, `drawLift` (stations, wheels, two cables, chairs, answer tiles over Kai's head). Normal jumps reach 4.9 tiles, and the trigger covers all the ground before the cliff, so it can't be skipped
