# Math Ninja notes: Training rooms (the dojo)

Part of the code notes; `CLAUDE.md` has the index. Search `index.html` for the names in backticks (line numbers drift).

## Training rooms / dojo drill (the door in each level; `relayoutVault` rebuilds the room for the new width when the phone turns, from the resize handler)

`MOVES` (15 moves, index world × 3 + level, `moveOf(def)`), `TRAIN_ROOM` names, `buildVault` (kind `'train'`, no blocks). **Fast drill:** Kai stands in stance facing Sensei's pad (locked in place until learned); answer with phone buttons (`miniTarget` kind `'train'`), keys , . / or clicking the big tiles (`drawDrill`, `room.tiles`, `cv` pointerdown). Right = Kai hits the pad (`TRAIN_HITS`, `room.hitT`), +1 gem, bar += `gain`; next problem at once. Wrong = Sensei says the right fact, next problem. **Anti-mash** (`TRAIN_STUN`, `TRAIN_READ`): taps in the first 0.4 s of a problem don't count (`room.qT`), a miss knocks the bar back 0.75 × gain and stuns Kai 1.2 / 2.5 / 4 s for misses in a row (`room.stun`, `room.wrun`; off-balance pose in `kaiPose`, tiles/buttons dimmed, `#ansbar.stun`), and a step / the finale only lands with ≤ 1 miss in the last 8 (`room.last`, else "Almost!"); "Breathe" ease only when ≥ 60% right. **Power bar** `room.bar` drains (`drain × ease`), never below a learned step; steps at 1/3, 2/3; full = finale. Needs `need = max(8, 0.8 × rate)` rights; `rate` (rights/min) from skill tier `[7,9,11,13,14]` or 0.9 × the kid's last average `prog.dojoRpm[skill]`; stuck low for 7 s → `ease` × 0.6 + "Breathe". `trainAnswer`, `updateRoom`. **Finale:** `trainPoseAt` (`TRAIN_SEGS` slow wind-up, fast strike), dim room, after-images (`drawPlayer`), speed lines, flash + shake + drum at `TRAIN_STRIKE`, name slams in (`drawTrain`), `trainFinish` (medal Gold/Silver/Bronze vs rate, `prog.moves`, gems). **Power on exit** (`leaveVault`): `G.power.kind = 'sensei'` 8 s = star + speed (+ super jump for kicks), Kai uses the move pose in the air (`kaiPose`). Poses: `drawKaiPose`/`mixPose`/`limbEnd`. **Pixel-art poses:** `posedFigure(F, pose)` builds the posed figure on the sprite's own cell grid and it is drawn like a sprite (cached per sprite set + rounded pose; never smooth lines or canvas rotation). Order, back to front: far arm `arm2` (suit's dark shade) behind the body; the body (`POSE_BODIES` `p0`–`p7`, baked in `bakeNinja` / `SENSEI_BODY` without the hands / front leg the pose draws, so no extra hands or third leg), leaned by `pose.r` after `scale2x` for clean edges (eye pixels `e` are put back one each after the lean, so a bow doesn't double them); then the kicking `leg` and near `arm` (Kai: suit's light shade). Limbs are 2 cells thick with a 1-cell outline; across the figure's own clothes the edge is softer (`F.inner`: a dark shade of Kai's suit via `darkCol`, so his limbs show against it (owner asked); grey for Sensei). Kai's shoulders/hips sit on his small body under the big head: `KAI_LIMBS` (shoulder 16 px, hip 8 px, limb lengths × 0.75), also used by `limbEnd`; Sensei `SENSEI_FIG` (× 0.9). `GUARD` (= `TRAIN_READY`): lead fist out front, rear fist peeking out by the chest. Lesson scroll Kai is zoomed 3× (whole number). Gate flourish `G.flourish`

## Dojo layout + effects

Kai and Sensei stand in the middle (`senseiC = cols/2 + 2`), boards on the left. Phones: answer buttons are a big centred row (`#ansbar.drill`, set in `syncAnsBar` when `t.kind === 'train'`): **upright phones put it in the empty space under the stage** (owner: buttons covered Sensei and the words), sideways at 26% height and a bit smaller, move pad hidden while drilling (`body.drilling`). Effects: combo words / "N combo!" over Kai (one at a time, `tag: 'combo'`), flame bits at 5+ combo, golden aura that grows with the bar, Sensei bounces on each hit, gong + small flash at each step

## Try the move

`moveNow`, `doMove`, `moveTick` (strike at frame 16: smashes `room.boards`, knocks out enemies), key X (`KMAP` → `keys.move`), phone button `.tbtn[data-k="move"]` shown via `body.canmove`.
